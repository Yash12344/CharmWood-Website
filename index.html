A Word of WelcomeEducation that begins with the child.

For more than two and a half decades, Charmwood has been a quiet, steady presence in Dehradun — a school where children are met first as children, and only then as students. We believe early learning is less about ticking syllabus boxes and more about lighting small fires of curiosity that will burn for a lifetime.

Our classrooms are warm, our teachers patient, and our days planned around the rhythms of young minds. Whether your child is just learning to hold a pencil or stepping confidently into Class 5, you will find here an environment that listens, encourages and gently challenges.

— Mrs. Ritu Sharma Principal, Charmwood International School
Our Guiding Values

Satya · Sneh · Sahas · Sanyam

Four ancient words that have shaped Indian thought for centuries — and four small lessons we hope every child carries home with them, every single day.

स
Satya

Truth

To speak honestly, to act honestly, and to live without pretence.

स्
Sneh

Love

To be kind to others, kind to oneself, and gentle with the world.

सा
Sahas

Courage

To try, to fall, to stand again — and to stand up for what is right.

सं
Sanyam

Patience

To wait, to listen, to think before acting — the quiet strength.

Why Charmwood

A school built around how children actually learn.

01

Low Student-Teacher Ratio

Average class size is kept close to 30, with attentive 1:30 supervision so every child is seen, heard and supported through their day.

02

ICSE Curriculum, Indian Roots

The rigour of the Indian School Certificate framework, taught with regional warmth — English, Hindi, Sanskrit, and our own cultural calendar.

03

Art, Music, Movement, Play

Dance, instrumental music, clay modelling, art & craft, outdoor games — held with the same seriousness as Mathematics and Language.

04

Language Lab & Library

A dedicated language lab to build confident speakers, and a well-stocked library where the habit of reading takes root early.

05

Safe & Familiar Campus

A compact, secure campus in Dalanwala with attentive staff, CCTV monitoring and a strict closed-gate policy through the school day.

06

Engaged Parent Community

Regular parent-teacher meets, open houses and small-group conversations — because a child's growth is a shared responsibility.
