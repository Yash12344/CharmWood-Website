Our Campus & Facilities

Everything a young learner needs, and nothing they don't.

A Tour

Built for primary learners.

Our campus has been designed with one user in mind — the young child. Furniture is the right size, corridors are well-lit, and every corner of the building is within sight of a teacher.

📚

Library

A warm, well-stocked library with picture books, early readers and reference titles for every age group. Reading periods are timetabled.

💻

Computer Lab

A fully equipped lab where children take their first steps with technology — with one device per student during lab periods.

📢

Language Lab

Audio-visual resources that help children listen, speak and read with confidence in English and Hindi.

🧪

Science Corner

A hands-on science space where curiosity meets equipment — magnets, magnifiers, plants, prisms, and lots of careful adult supervision.

🎨

Art & Craft Room

A bright studio for drawing, painting, clay modelling and paper craft — with materials laid out and aprons within reach.

🎼

Music Room

An attractive room for vocal music, harmonium, casio and percussion lessons — the heart of our cultural calendar.

💃

Dance Studio

A mirrored studio with sprung flooring for classical, folk and creative-movement classes.

⚽

Sports & Play Area

An open play area for outdoor games, athletics and free play — plus indoor games on rainy days.

🚌

Transport

School transport on key Dehradun routes, with a trained attendant on each bus and live tracking for parents.

🍽

Auditorium

A multipurpose auditorium for assemblies, performances, parent meetings and our annual cultural events.

👨‍⚕️

Health & First Aid

A first-aid room staffed during school hours, with a visiting paediatrician for regular health check-ups.

🔒

Safety & Security

CCTV across the campus, a strict closed-gate policy, signed-in visitors and trained staff supervising every transition.
