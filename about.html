About the School

A small school with deep roots in Dehradun and a clear idea of what early education ought to look like.

Our StoryA quiet beginning, in 1998.

Charmwood was founded in 1998 in Dalanwala, Dehradun, by a small group of educators who shared a single conviction — that the first years of school are the most important years of all. The school began modestly, with a handful of children, a borrowed piano, and a deep belief in doing the basics well.

More than two decades on, the school has grown but our intent has not changed. We remain a primary school by choice, not by accident. The years from Playgroup to Class 5 demand a different kind of attention — smaller spaces, slower pace, gentler hands — and we have built ourselves entirely around that idea.

Today, Charmwood is affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (ICSE), New Delhi, and serves children from across Dehradun. Many of our alumni now bring their own children to our gates, which we consider the highest compliment a school can receive.

What We Stand For

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a school where children grow into honest, thoughtful and capable human beings — rooted in the wisdom of our culture and open to the possibilities of the world.

Our Mission

To deliver a balanced education that develops the head, the heart and the hand — through a strong academic core, a rich co-curricular life, and an everyday culture built on the values of Satya, Sneh, Sahas and Sanyam.

Principal's Message"Let us not rush them. Let us walk with them."

Every September, when our new little ones arrive at the gate, I am reminded why this school was started. A child who is met with patience in their first years carries that patience into adulthood. A child who is listened to learns to listen. A child who is gently corrected learns to correct themselves.

At Charmwood, we have resisted the temptation to grow too fast or to chase too many trends. We do a few things, and we try to do them well: read aloud to children, count with them, sing with them, take them outdoors, teach them to apologise and to forgive, and prepare them for the years of schooling that lie ahead.

If you are considering Charmwood for your child, do come and visit us. A school is best understood by walking its corridors.

— Mrs. Ritu Sharma Principal
