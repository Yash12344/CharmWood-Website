Academics

An ICSE-aligned curriculum, carefully paced and gently delivered — from playgroup through upper primary.

Our Approach

Three principles that shape every classroom.

01

Children First, Syllabus Second

We teach to the child in front of us, not to the timetable. A topic stays on the board until it has been understood — not just covered.

02

Concrete Before Abstract

Especially in Mathematics and Science, we begin with objects, hands and stories — and only then move to symbols, formulae and definitions.

03

Reading, Always Reading

A daily reading habit is the single most important academic investment we make. Every class reads aloud, every day, without exception.

AssessmentHow we measure learning.

We follow the ICSE assessment framework with continuous and comprehensive evaluation. The intent is not to rank children against one another but to give each child — and each parent — an honest picture of what is going well and what needs more time.

  • Two terminal examinations each year (Classes 1 – 5)
  • Periodic class assessments and oral work
  • Observational reports for Pre-Primary
  • Detailed report cards with descriptive feedback
  • One-to-one parent meetings each term
