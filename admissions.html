Admissions 2026 – 27

We welcome a small, intentional intake each year — so that every child finds the attention they deserve.

EligibilityWho can apply?

Admissions are offered from Playgroup through Class 5. The age criteria below are indicative and reflect the age the child must have completed on or before 31 March of the academic session.

ClassMinimum Age
Playgroup2 years
Nursery3 years
Kindergarten (KG)4 years
Class 15+ years
Classes 2 – 5Age appropriate to class

Final eligibility is decided by the school after the child-parent interaction.

The Process

Four small steps to begin.

Collect Form

Pick up the registration form from the school office, or download it from this page.

Submit Documents

Return the form with the required documents and registration fee.

Interaction

A short, friendly conversation with the child and the parents.

Confirmation

An admission offer is communicated, followed by fee payment and joining details.

What to BringDocuments required

  • Completed registration form
  • Birth certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Four recent passport-size photographs of the child
  • Aadhaar card of the child (if available)
  • Address proof of the parents
  • Transfer Certificate — for Class 1 onwards, if applying from another school
  • Previous class report card — for Class 2 onwards
  • Medical record / vaccination card
